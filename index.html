VOICE

Downloads once, then works offline. On a phone each reply takes a second or two to generate before she speaks.

Voices come from your system. On Android, Chrome picks the voice by accent: set Accent here, and choose which British or American voice it uses in your phone's text-to-speech settings. On Windows, the “Microsoft” and “Google UK English” voices sound the most composed.
